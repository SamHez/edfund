Rebecca Day — full profile

Rebecca Day

Director and Education Consultant

After thirty-three years in United States education with twenty years as a classroom teacher, eight years as an Assistant Principal and Principal, and five years as Founder and CEO of Health Solutions Associates, I am pleased to be a Director of EdFund Uganda Foundation to support teachers and administrators in their quest to educate children in Uganda. Upon leaving public school administration in 2017, I founded Health Solutions Associates and designed and teach the H.E.A.R.T. Method of Self Care for teachers and administrators as a precise formula for revitalizing their energy, health, and passion for the work they love to do … TEACH AND EDUCATE CHILDREN!

In 2019, I joined EdFund Uganda Foundation, a non-profit organization supporting schools in Eastern Uganda, by helping teachers develop 21st century education curriculum via technology use with their students. This work has profoundly impacted me and my perspective on the importance of continuing to give to others. I have a B.S. in Health and Physical Education from the University of Vermont and a M. Ed from Springfield College with a focus in Athletic Administration. My advanced degree work is in Educational Leadership leading to my principal licensure, which I still hold. My passions as a health educator and school principal led me to where I am today … a person deeply concerned for the well being of teachers and administrators all over the world. I am very excited to be a part of the EdFund Uganda Foundation team bringing my experience and love for education to the schools in Eastern Uganda.

John C. Giebink — full profile

John C. Giebink

Founder and Executive Director

EdFund Uganda Foundation was originally founded by Mr. Giebink to construct projects in the District of Kamuli Uganda which would have an immediate impact for school children and rural poor families. The mission of EdFund Uganda Foundation is to invest in the schools of Eastern Uganda to enhance the opportunity and accessibility of education and technology for youth and community, as a means for empowering productive citizenship. The future of Uganda and Africa is inextricably linked to improving family health, welfare, education, and technology in the rural areas. Prior to founding EdFund Uganda Foundation in 2015, Mr. Giebink completed numerous construction projects in the Kamuli Uganda District over the preceding 8 years including energy efficient kitchens in rural villages, a commercial energy efficient kitchen and dining hall at a primary school with 2,000 students and initiating internship programs in Eastern Uganda for United States university students. In 2015, Mr. Giebink formed a partnership with KAPIDA (see Mr. Dhizaala’a profile) to specifically rebuild the Kamuli Township Primary School with over 2,300 students in grades P1 to P7. Over the past 10 years, EdFund Uganda Foundation has funded and KAPIDA has managed the re-building and new construction of 8 buildings including: a dining hall and commercial energy-efficient kitchen for feeding 2,000 students daily, 3 classroom buildings, an administration building, a community center for the school and town (population of 100,000), a girls’ dormitory for 100 students, a boys dormitory for 100 students, and new sanitary facilities for all the bildings.

A 1977 graduate of Middlebury College with a BA and High Honors in Economics, Mr. Giebink founded a full-service construction and real estate company working in New England and upstate New York. Mr. Giebink now has over 50 years of experience in designing and constructing a wide variety of projects - from luxury single family homes to residential housing developments containing several hundred units each. Over the past 30 years, he has focused on developing, constructing and managing new mixed income housing projects, specifically those in the Low-Income Tax Credit Program. Over the course of his career, he has secured funding through numerous housing programs for over 20 affordable housing projects with 1,500 units throughout VT, NH, and up-state NY. John is primarily responsible for the financial and legal matters related to the various companies and properties in Summit Property’s portfolio, and for oversight of design, permitting and construction of new projects.

As EdFund Uganda Foundation evolves into the 21st century, its mission has expanded into the world of technology with a focus on bringing computer education, internet, and technology resources to students, teachers, administrators, and community members in Eastern Uganda. To that end, EdFund has completed a Technology Center at the Kamuli Township Primary School with 41 computers interconnected to each other, to the internet, to a projection system, and to a sound system. The intent is to educate and train students, teachers and community members in the interconnectivity of the new world. With future funding, EdFund hopes to establish additional technology centers in the Kamuli District and Eastern Uganda.

Dhizaala Stephen — full profile

Dhizaala Stephen

Director of Education and Facilities Management

Dhizaala John Stephen born in 1948 as the 3rd child in a less privileged family of 5, is a life-long Ugandan citizen working in the promotion of education for children and young adults. He started school at age 10 and worked his way through the Uganda education system from primary school to receiving a graduate degree in 1968 from St. John Bosco – Nyondo Primary Teachers College in Eastern Uganda as a Grade II primary school Teacher. In 1983, he returned to college for upgrading to get a Grade III primary school teacher certificate. During this period, he taught school in the Mbale District and then back to Kamuli Home District teaching in the schools of St. Theresa Girls Day and Boarding School, Kamuli Township Primary and later promoted to hierarchy of Headmaster Grade Two in Kiinu Primary whereby he retired from the civil service in 1991.

In 2002, the Uganda Ministry of Education together with Busoga Kingdom called him back to serve as Tutor in Busoga Teachers Training at Iganga Core Primary Teachers College, Kaliro Teachers Primary Teachers Training College, and Wanyange Primary Teachers Training College. His major teaching areas involved Development of Lusoga Language and Literature together with reviving Busoga Cultural Norms and Traditions up to the university level. In 2006 he joined the Kyambogo National Curriculum Development Centre as a Uganda Schools Curriculum panel member in all sects from primary to university levels of education which he continues to serve on today.

In 2005, Stephen with his wife Josephine founded Kamuli Area Peoples Integral Development Association (KAPIDA). As a registered Ugandan NGO, it works to promote education and sanitary infrastructure work in rural areas surrounding Kamuli, Uganda. While Josephine died in 2023, Stephen continues this very important work with his daughter, Betty. In addition, his son, Kaloli, was elected Governor of the Kamuli District (population 500,000) in 2026.

As Director of Education and Facilities Management for EdFund Uganda Foundation, he oversees all design and construction of facilities and all communications with local and District Education Officials. He is hands-on with procuring all materials and vendors for EdFund’s development work and with planning for EdFund’s future projects He works tirelessly and lovingly to support less privileged families and education institutions in a District with a population of 550,000. Through KAPIDA, he continues to support local community livelihood, education and health promotion programs.

Robert Ssebuliba

Director of Operations

Ssebuliba Robert is a results-driven operations and IT professional with over 10 years of experience with Uganda Development Services where he played a key role in strengthening organizational operations through project implementation, monitoring and evaluation, reporting, and systems development. He has consistently demonstrated the ability to align operational processes with strategic goals, ensuring smooth program execution and measurable impact. He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology and Computing, a Diploma in Computer Science, and a Certificate in Computer Science from Kyambogo University, providing him with a strong academic foundation to support both technical and operational leadership.

As Director of Operations for EdFund Uganda Foundation, he oversees the design, implementation, and management of technology-driven operational systems to enhance organizational efficiency and impact including IT infrastructure, database systems, network operations, and digital platforms, ensuring reliability, security, and scalability. He plays a critical role in developing and enforcing operational policies, strengthening systems, and integrating technology into education and community development programs. He also supports organizational strategy through budgeting, resource planning, donor engagement, and performance monitoring.

Robert is a strong leader with proven experience in team supervision, capacity building, and stakeholder engagement. Known for being innovative, adaptable, and highly motivated, he excels at managing complex workloads, improving systems, and driving organizational success. He is also skilled in public speaking, community mobilization, and training, enabling him to drive both internal efficiency and external impact. Fluent in English and Luganda, with good proficiency in Lusoga, he effectively engages diverse communities and stakeholders.
