Learning with Confidence

16 September 2026

Starting with the basics

Nabakazi Petralina, a teacher at St. Mulumba Primary School in Kamuli District, describes beginning the training with little computer experience. She learned to operate a computer, type and save her work.

Taking new skills back to school

Her account describes using Word and Excel to prepare lesson plans and report cards, using email and finding information online. She plans to keep practising and share what she has learned with fellow teachers.
