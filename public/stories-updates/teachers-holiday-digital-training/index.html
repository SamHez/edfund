Digital Teacher Training

16 September 2026

Practical skills for everyday teaching

The Giebink Technology Center at Township Primary School hosted a digital training programme during the August–September 2026 school holiday. Sessions focused on using technology in teaching and school administration.

From lesson plans to digital records

Participants explored digital lesson plans, PowerPoint presentations, report cards and internet research. The training offered time to practise computer skills that teachers can apply in their daily work.
