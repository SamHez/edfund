Community Digital Skills

16 September 2026

A new opportunity to learn

In June 2026, the Giebink Technology Center began a community digital training programme in Kamuli. Its first initiative focused on digital skills for young people.

Skills connected to local ambitions

The programme was designed to help participants use practical technology skills to develop their business ideas. It extends the centre’s learning opportunities beyond the school community.
