Our Work

Education, technology and community support that connect to everyday life in Kamuli.

education

Spaces where learning can thrive.

EdFund supports the renovation and construction of facilities at Kamuli Township Primary School. Its work includes classroom buildings, school accommodation and shared facilities that support a safer learning environment.

Read the latest stories ↗

technology

Technology within reach.

The Giebink Technology Center brings computer and internet access to Township Primary School. Training supports teaching, school administration and practical digital learning for young people and community members.

Read the latest stories ↗

community

Learning that reaches beyond the classroom.

EdFund works with KAPIDA in Kamuli. Teacher development, community digital training and shared learning spaces connect the school’s facilities with the ambitions of the wider community.
